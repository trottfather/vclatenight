Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information VC Late Night (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit vclatenight.com, and what we do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Information you give us

Newsletter. Our newsletter runs on Substack. When you subscribe, you enter your email address on Substack, not on this site. Substack shares your subscription details with us so we can send you the newsletter. Substack’s handling of your information is covered by the Substack Privacy Policy. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email.

Contacting us or booking a call. If you email us or book a call through our scheduling link (provided by Google Calendar), we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, email address and message. We use them only to reply to you.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visited. We use this to keep the site running, secure and to understand overall traffic. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not sell it.

Videos and other services

Our videos are hosted on YouTube. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play; when you do, the video plays in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and YouTube’s own privacy policy applies. Links to TikTok, Instagram, X, YouTube and Substack take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party services you choose to use (such as YouTube or Substack) may set their own cookies.

How we use and share information

  • To send the newsletter you asked for.
  • To reply to messages and booking requests.
  • To operate, secure and improve the site.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us do the things above, or when required by law.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
